# Xona Space Systems

> Xona Space Systems builds Pulsar, a commercial low-Earth-orbit (LEO) positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT) satellite service designed to complement and harden GPS with centimeter-level accuracy and encrypted, jam-resistant signals. Xona publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or developer portal as of July 2026; its ecosystem is delivered through receiver, simulator, and test-platform partners under the Pulsar Verified program.

Xona Space Systems is a Craft Ventures portfolio company headquartered in Burlingame, California. The Pulsar service targets a constellation of 258 small satellites at roughly 1,080 km altitude, and is designed so existing GNSS devices can adopt it via software/firmware updates from ecosystem partners (Trimble, Septentrio, Topcon, Furuno, Murata, Locus Lock, and others). Serves defense, agriculture, telecom, transportation, infrastructure, maritime, aviation, and autonomous systems.
